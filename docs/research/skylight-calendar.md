# Skylight Calendar: what it shows, how it's used, what reviewers say

Research note, 2026-10-29. Question: what does the Skylight Calendar (Cal 10 in and 15 in, Calendar 2 15 in, Cal Max 27 in) show and do, and what can a self-hosted Home Assistant wall board take from it? Companion to [wall-display-legibility.md](wall-display-legibility.md).

Lines marked **[inference]** are my own judgement, not something a source states. Skylight changes the product often: its help centre has dated "[Feature]" posts through October 2026. Dates are given for each source. "Help centre" means Skylight's Zendesk at skylight.zendesk.com.

**Sources not reached:** Wirecutter (nytimes.com), CNET and Reddit block both fetch tools used here, and no CNET or Tom's Guide full review of the Calendar turned up. Searches for owner complaints mostly returned spam mirror pages, which this note leaves out. The owner-voice material below therefore comes from reviewers and one sync-tool vendor, not from Reddit.

## 2. What the home screen shows

**The Home Screen** (help centre, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/49738702477723-The-Home-Screen, fetched 2026-10-29) has three panes:
- **Calendar pane:** set to Schedule, Day, Week or Month view. Day view was added 2026-08-06 ("[Feature] The Calendar Home Screen Supports Day View"); Schedule and Month were added later ("[Feature] Schedule and Month View in Calendar Home", https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/55564416758427).
- **Tasks pane:** today's tasks per profile, "Swipe left or right to show more Profiles"; tasks can be ticked off there.
- **Lists pane:** the account's lists, swipe for more, "Add item" under each.
- Tasks and Lists can each be hidden ("Customize"), so the home screen can be calendar-only. "Home Screen will always show your weekly events" ("[Feature] Home Screen", updated 2026-04-29, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/49738858986907).
- An **Add** button adds an event or task.

**The Calendar tab** (help centre, "The Calendar Tab", https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/36625171368987):
- Four views: **Schedule** ("a column layout for today and upcoming days"; pinch to widen the time span), **Day** (grid plus a list of the day's events), **Week** (grid), **Month** (standard grid).
- **Now marker:** today's date has an orange dot and the current time is an orange bar across the timeline.
- **Filter button:** toggles task progress, weather, planned meals, and each profile on or off (plus "Select All").
- **Weather:** forecast icons on each day for the next ten days in Week and Month views, once an address is set. Product page also claims a forecast "at the time and location of your event" (myskylight.com product page, 2026-10). Reviewed.com (2026-07-08) says the top of the calendar shows the current time and outside temperature: https://www.reviewed.com/smarthome/content/skylight-calendar-2-review
- Navigation: swipe left and right for dates, up and down for time; "Next Week" and "Today" buttons.
- **No "up next" or "right now" panel** is described in any help article or review found. The only "now" cue is the orange time bar, plus optional countdowns (below).

**Colour coding and profiles:**
- Each family member is a profile with its own colour; "Assign each family member their own color" is a free feature (product page). Unlimited profiles (Taste of Home, 2026-06-07, https://www.tasteofhome.com/article/skylight-calendar-review/).
- Since 2025-10-29 the profile picture defaults to the person's **initial**; you can pick a stock avatar or upload a photo, and choose a preset or custom colour ("[Feature] Customize Your Profile Picture and Color", https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/42815603725979).
- Setting "Color code multi-profile events" shows a shared event with each profile's colour (help centre "Calendar Settings", updated 2026-07-15, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/36835449004315).

**Meals and lists:**
- Meal planning (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) is a **Plus** feature; you choose which meal categories show on the calendar. One reviewer shows only dinner (Reviewed.com, 2026-07-08). Meals are also a separate tab (Verge, 2025-02).
- Lists (grocery, to-do) are **free**. They don't sync with outside list apps; reviewers flag this (Verge 2025-02; Reviewed.com 2026-07). Lists "pop up over the calendar view" (Verge photo caption, 2025-02).

## 3. Layouts, days shown, text size, glanceability

- **Days in Schedule view:** a slider, 1 to 7 days; landscape shows the most ("How do I view more days on the 'Schedule' view", updated 2024-05-01, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059869651; "Calendar Settings", 2026-07-15). No default is documented.
- **Other view settings** ("Calendar Settings", 2026-07-15): start week on Sunday or Monday; Week view "Start on current day" (rolling week); Month view "Start on current week"; Schedule view "Start on current day"; **Shade weekends**; **Dim past events**; **Show countdowns** (always, 3 months before, or 1 month before an event); a calendar display name in place of the date.
- **Text size:** three sizes, small, medium or large, set under General Settings ("How do I increase the font size", updated 2026-10-26, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083058580763). The help centre doesn't say which text it scales. Product page lists "Customized Views" as free.
- **Density on 15 in:** "can show five to seven days and about an eight-hour day in time slots" (Verge, 2024-01-30, https://www.theverge.com/2024/1/30/24054438/skylight-cal-max-smart-family-calendar-launch-pricing). Her later complaint: the 15 in "just wasn't large enough to display four people's schedules at a glance, and the low-quality display was hard to see at some angles" (Verge, 2025-02-20).
- **Month view is cramped:** "The month view is the weak spot for the Skylight" (Reviewed.com, 2026-07-08). The same reviewer's favourite is Day view; she hides tasks from the main calendar because they crowd it.
- **Across the room:** on the 27 in, larger text "is easier to read from afar" and viewing angles are much better than the 15 in (GadgetGuy, 2025-08-07, https://www.gadgetguy.com.au/skylight-calendar-max-review). Verge's Cal Max: "sharp enough to make out the calendar text clearly from most angles" with nine calendars on screen; a teen said "I like how easy it is to just look over and see what I have on today" (2025-02-20). No source measures reading distance.
- **[inference]** The 15 in (1920 x 1080 on about 13 x 7.4 in) shows roughly what a 14 in 1200 x 800 CSS-px tablet can, so the 15 in density figures (5 to 7 days, about 8 hours) are the useful benchmark for this board, not the Max.

## 4. Adding and editing events

- **On the device:** tap the Add button (a "big purple plus icon at the bottom right of the calendar screen", Android Police, 2024-07-06, https://www.androidpolice.com/skylight-calendar-review/), then type with an on-screen keyboard. The Add dialog offers **Type** (default), **Photo**, **Talk** and **Email** (help centre "The Calendar Tab"). Tapping an event opens a details panel (title, time, reminder, profiles, source calendar, invitees, notes).
- **Reviewers prefer the phone app for entry:** the on-device keyboard is "rather cumbersome compared to doing it on your computer, smartphone or tablet" (GadgetGuy, 2025-08-07); "I found the app much quicker and easier to use when entering information" (Taste of Home, 2026-06-07).
- **Photo, Talk (voice) and Email go through Sidekick / Magic Import**, which "is only available with Calendar Plus subscription" ("Import Calendar Events", updated 2026-07-23, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/39335538450587). Talk records a spoken description and returns a draft to confirm. Email: forward to the device's address; putting a profile name in the subject assigns the event; a confirmation email follows. Verge found Sidekick parsed a multi-day tournament flyer well and emails back a list with a delete button per entry (2025-02-20).
- **Free email-in:** invite the calendar's own email address as an attendee (product page).
- **Sync:** imports Google, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, Cozi, TeamSnap and any public calendar link. **Only Google Calendar syncs two ways**; an event added on the device to another service's calendar stays on Skylight only (Verge, 2025-02-20).

## 5. What reviewers praise and complain about

**Praise**
- Easy setup and use (Verge 2025-02; Taste of Home 2026-06; Reviewed.com 2026-07).
- One shared, colour-coded place the whole household actually looks at (Verge 2025-02; Taste of Home 2026-06).
- No voice assistant or apps to distract (Verge 2025-02).
- Calendar, colour coding, lists and sleep mode work without a subscription (Taste of Home 2026-06).
- Google sync quick and two-way (Reviewed.com 2026-07); "feels instant" from the app (Android Police, 2026-04-09, https://www.androidpolice.com/i-hate-smart-home-tech-but-the-skylight-calendar-2-is-an-exception/).

**Complaints**
- **Subscription paywall:** Plus went from $39 to $79 a year the day after the Verge review (Verge, 2025-02-21, https://www.theverge.com/news/617248/skylight-doubled-its-subscription-price-after-we-published-our-review). $79 is still the US price on myskylight.com (2026-10). Behind it: meal planning, recipes, photo screensaver, Magic Import / Sidekick (including Talk and Photo entry), rewards, Disney Mode ("Calendar Plus", help centre, updated 2026-10-16, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/57326775089691). "Many features are locked behind a paywall"; scored 6/10 (Android Police, 2024-07-06).
- **Readability and density on 15 in:** too small for four people's schedules; poor viewing angles (Verge 2025-02). Month view cramped (Reviewed.com 2026-07).
- **Too bright at night, no dark mode:** "despite auto-brightness, the screen is a bit too bright at night ... a dark mode would be very welcome" (Verge 2025-02).
- **Screensaver hides the calendar:** in photo-frame mode "the calendar isn't glanceable", you must tap to see your schedule (GadgetGuy 2025-08). Skylight's later fixes, both Plus-only: weather and upcoming events overlaid on the screensaver (2026-08-26, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/54985620902171) and a schedule of times when the screensaver stays off, such as mornings and dinner prep (2026-10-13, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/57164276523163). The screensaver doesn't pull from Google Photos (Verge 2025-02).
- **Sync lag and gaps:** non-Google sources are one-way and refresh "at intervals", so changes may arrive late or not at all; deleting several events on the device doesn't always work (CalendarBridge, a competing sync vendor, so biased; 2025-12-11, updated 2026-07-24, https://calendarbridge.com/blog/how-to-fix-sync-issues-on-the-most-popular-digital-family-calendars/). No review measured sync lag.
- **On-device entry clumsy** (GadgetGuy 2025-08). **Lists sandboxed** (Verge 2025-02; Reviewed.com 2026-07). **Price** (every review). **Vendor risk:** if the company folds you may have "a big, defunct screen" (Verge 2025-02).

**Sleep schedule:** free. Set "From" and "To" times under Sleep, or "Enter Sleep Mode Now"; a tap wakes it ("Sleep Mode", updated 2026-10-26, https://skylight.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/37235485034779). Reviewers call it helpful (Verge 2025-02; Taste of Home 2026-06).

## 6. A self-hosted look-alike exists

A Home Assistant "Skylight Calendar Card" (first posted 2026-01-31) offers Month, Week and Schedule views, `rolling_days` (today plus N days), a current-time bar, dark mode and event create/edit/delete. Thread: https://community.home-assistant.io/t/skylight-calendar-card-a-family-friendly-schedule-card/981221 ; code: https://github.com/superdingo101/skylight-calendar-card . Not reviewed here; listed only as a reference for layout ideas, not as a suggestion to install.

## 7. Ideas for a self-hosted Home Assistant wall board on a 14-inch 1200x800 CSS-px tablet read from 6-12 ft, two adults, no kids (no chores/rewards)

Ranked by value for effort. All are **[inference]**, drawn from the findings above. Check what the card already does before building any of them; prefer a setting or Home Assistant configuration over code.

1. **Rolling days that start today, about 4 to 5 days, not a calendar week.** Skylight's most-used settings are "Start on current day" and a 1 to 7 day Schedule slider; its 15 in fits 5 to 7 days and about 8 hours. With two people, 4 to 5 columns at 1200 px leaves room for legible text (see the legibility note). Yesterday is wasted space on a glance board.
2. **Dim past events today, keep a strong "now" line.** Skylight ships both ("Dim past events", orange time bar). From 10 ft, dimming the morning's finished items makes the next thing the brightest thing on the board, which does the job of an "up next" panel without adding one.
3. **Night: dark or off on a schedule.** The Verge's chief display complaint was a screen too bright at night with no dark mode. Do it in Home Assistant, not the card: a schedule that blanks or dims the tablet overnight and wakes on touch or motion, and optionally a dark theme in the evening.
4. **Show a shared event in both people's colours.** Skylight's "Color code multi-profile events". With two adults, "both of us" is a common case and should read differently from "one of us" at a glance.
5. **Don't use Month as the resting view.** Reviewers find it cramped even on 15 in. Keep Month one tap away for planning, and keep the resting view to the rolling days.
6. **Weather as one icon and high/low per day column**, not a separate panel. Skylight puts forecast icons on each day; that answers "do I need a coat on Thursday?" without spending vertical space.
7. **Dinner only on the calendar.** Skylight lets you pick which meal categories show; reviewers show only dinner. One meal line per day is glanceable; four is clutter.
8. **Don't invest in typing on the wall.** Every reviewer who compared found phone entry faster than the on-screen keyboard. Keep entry on phones and voice (the house already has Assist scripts for adding events and notes); the board's job is reading.
9. **Countdown for one or two big dates** (trip, visit, holiday). Skylight's "Show countdowns" with a 1- or 3-month window. Cheap, and from across the room it reads as a single number.
10. **No photo screensaver.** Skylight's own reviewers found it hides the calendar, and Skylight then added weather and events on top of it and a schedule to suppress it. A board that always shows the calendar avoids the problem.
