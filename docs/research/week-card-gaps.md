# What the week card does that the house board won't

Research note, 2026-11-01, for [What does the week card do that the house board won't?](https://github.com/bellamy1506/HA-Family-Hub-Clover/issues/95), on the [map](https://github.com/bellamy1506/HA-Family-Hub-Clover/issues/37). It lists everything a person can see or do on today's boards, so nothing goes unnoticed when the week card retires. The keep-or-drop calls belong to the owner at [What from the week card does the house keep, and what does it let go?](https://github.com/bellamy1506/HA-Family-Hub-Clover/issues/98); what runs behind the week card is [#96](https://github.com/bellamy1506/HA-Family-Hub-Clover/issues/96).

**Method.** I read the code Home Assistant serves on `main` at `eee6eb9`: `custom_components/family_hub/card/family-week-calendar-card.js` ("card" below, 27,022 lines) and `custom_components/family_hub/__init__.py` ("init"). The card's hash, `8629914459`, matches the `?v=` the house serves, so this is the installed card. Line numbers are in those files at that commit. The house's setup was read through the Home Assistant connector, read only, on 2026-11-01: the `/family-hub` dashboard and a search of every dashboard, `family_hub/get_settings`, `family_hub/device_settings/list`, Family Hub's integration options, its stores (recipes, suggestions, meal ideas, "Also for" tags, checklists, alert choices, themes), the to-do counts and the meal plan's items. Nothing was changed. Decisions come from the map's Decisions so far and the prototype READMEs they link. *(inferred)* marks my judgment.

## Answer

The decided house board covers nearly everything the house uses. **Nothing decides what happens to these:** the **Daily Digest**, **Print** and **Templates** (the three the owner named), **changing or clearing a planned dinner**, and **an event's place and notes** in its sheet. There's also a handful of extras nobody has used: countdowns, checklists on events, "Also for" tags, "Use as meal", extra meal slots, and Debug Info for sessions.

The three named ones are smaller than they look:
- **On the phones the week card has no More menu.** Every phone copy uses the "Title only" top bar, so Print, Templates and the Daily Digest are reachable only at the wall.
- **No templates are saved,** and the meal plan has no items at all, so there's nothing to save yet.
- **The Daily Digest never shows events at the house.** It takes them from Family Hub's "Calendars to monitor", which is empty. It shows only notes due today and today's meals, and the house board's front page already shows both, with the day's events too.
- **Print likely does nothing here** *(inferred)*. It opens a new window and the browser's print dialog, which Fully on the wall may ignore, and no printer is recorded at the house.

My recommendations for the owner: drop all three; give the house board a small dinner sheet (change the dish, clear it, Undo); show an event's place and notes in its sheet; drop the unused extras.

## Who sees what today

- **The wall** (Clover Tablet, signed in as Clover, not an admin) has the full top bar. It opens in Week: 4 days from today, the hour timeline, the Liquid Glass Tablet theme. It reaches Settings, the Week, Day and Month switch, Edit (rearrange meals), the arrows and jump-to-week, More (Templates, Print, Shopping List, Daily Digest, Enable Privacy Mode) and the + menu. Settings' Developer tools tab, with Debug Info and Discreet mode, is for admins only (card 12791-12792).
- **The phones:** three iPhone copies, all set to the "Title only" top bar (card 10844-10856). That bar shows only the month name: you swipe to page, and Settings moves into the + menu. With no view switch, they stay in the household's default view, Month, which these copies show as Split (a month grid with the tapped day's list). Split shows no meals, because Show meal plan in month view is off (card 25749). Two copies report Will's sign-in, and one (last seen Oct 26) has none recorded. No copy reports Jenn's, so whether Jenn uses the board on her phone is unknown.
- **The `/family-hub` dashboard:** page 1 holds two copies of the week card (with and without Work) and the Hide work calendars tile. Page 2 is Home Assistant's own To-do card for House reminders. No other Family Hub card is on any dashboard. Theme Builder is a separate sidebar page.
- **What's in use (2026-11-01):** the meal plan has no items, open or done. There are no meal templates, recipes, suggestions or meal ideas. Us notes and House reminders have nothing open (the reminder sync isn't built). Family Hub's stores for "Also for" tags, checklists, alerts set from an event's sheet, and countdowns are all empty.
